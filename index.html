The same swap, again and again

Match-3 boards tend to blur together: swap, match, repeat, and the only thing that changes from level to level is how long it takes.

Then the waiting starts

Timers, lives that refill in half an hour, ads between levels. You end up waiting more than you play.

Here, every swap turns the paper

In Washi Turn both sheets turn over when you swap them, so you read the board by what lies under each fold. No ads, no lives, no timers.

How it plays

The fold decides

Every sheet shows its face and one folded-up corner. Swap two neighbours and both turn over: line up three by the colour under the corner, not the colour on top.

Readable for everyone

Vermilion, saffron, matcha, indigo, plum and ash each carry their own washi print, so colour-blind players can tell every sheet apart.

Obstacles and tools

Crumpled paper balls and washi tape block the way. Scissors cut one cell, the Bone Folder turns one sheet over where it lies, and the Paperweight keeps your next swap face up.

Yours, offline

No account and no tracking. Progress stays on your iPhone and the game plays offline.

Paper that does things

FanLine up exactly four. It turns over every sheet in its row and column.
PinwheelMake an L or a T. It clears the 3×3 around it.
CraneLine up five. Swap it with any sheet to clear every sheet of its new face.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. You earn Clips from first clears and stars, and spend them on tools or extra turns. Three purchases are optional: Jar of Clips ($1.99), Paper Sets ($2.99, three extra looks with the same rules) and Clip Magnet ($3.99, doubles the Clips you earn from levels).

Are there ads, lives or timers?

No. Play as long as you like.

Does it collect any data?

No sign-in, no analytics, no tracking. Progress is stored on your device only. See the privacy policy.

Which devices?

iPhone, in English. It is coming soon to the App Store.
