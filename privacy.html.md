# Washi Turn Privacy Policy

Source: https://nguyenvanlamm.github.io/washi-turn-site/privacy.html

Washi Turn · last updated 2026-11-07

Washi Turn is a puzzle game. It does not ask who you are, and it does not sell any data about you.

## What stays on your device

- Your progress: levels cleared and their stars, your Clips and your tools (Scissors, Bone Folder, Paperweight).
- Your settings: the paper look you chose.
- Whether you have bought Paper Sets or Clip Magnet, and which App Store purchases have already been delivered.

This is stored on your device only. Deleting the app removes it.

## What we do not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising and no ad networks.
- No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
- No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

## Purchases

The game and all 80 levels are free. Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store under its own privacy policy. We receive no payment details; the game only learns from the store which purchases exist.

## Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
